About these returns and positions

Time-weighted return (TWR) adjusts for money entering or leaving an account. Money-weighted return (XIRR) reflects the timing of that money. Annualized returns express the measured period as a yearly rate.

Cost basis belongs to the open positions. Unrealized gain is their value less that basis; realized gain comes from closed lots. Open lots and P&L board are available at the latest snapshot.
